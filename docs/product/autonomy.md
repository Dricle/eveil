# Autonomy levels

Autonomy decides how much Eveil does on its own. Each channel has its own setting, under Settings → Autonomy, so you can let email run by itself while LinkedIn still waits for you.

## Email

"You" means you click a button, "Eveil" means it happens by itself.

| | 🟢 Supervised | 🟡 Semi-auto | 🔴 Autonomous |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Looking for new companies** | You click "search" | Eveil keeps looking by itself | Eveil keeps looking by itself |
| **Choosing which companies to email** | You say yes or no to each one | You say yes or no to each one | Eveil emails every company it finds that looks like a good fit |
| **Writing the emails** | You create the campaign | You create the campaign | Eveil writes a campaign for each target profile by itself |
| **Adding people to a campaign** | You click "start" or "run again" | Eveil adds them by itself once you've said yes to their company | Eveil adds them by itself |
| **Sending** | Eveil sends | Eveil sends | Eveil sends |

In one sentence each:

- **Supervised:** nothing happens until you click.
- **Semi-auto:** you approve companies, Eveil emails the people there by itself after that.
- **Autonomous:** Eveil does everything, and you just read the replies.

What never changes: in every mode, emails only go out from mailboxes you've connected, and anything you've set aside stays set aside.

## LinkedIn

| | 🟢 Supervised | 🔴 Autonomous |
|---|---|---|
| **Publishing a post** | You approve each one | Eveil publishes it as soon as it's written |

Two things still wait for you in Autonomous: a post that names a client (only the anonymous version goes out on its own), and any post on a project with more than one LinkedIn account, since Eveil can't guess which one to post as. There's no Semi-auto here: publishing is the only step, so there's nothing in between. New projects start on Supervised. See [Posting to LinkedIn](/product/linkedin).

## Reddit

Always supervised. Eveil drafts replies and you post them yourself, because posting on its own could get your account banned. See [Replying on Reddit](/product/reddit).
