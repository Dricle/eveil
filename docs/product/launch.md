# Launching your product

Getting a product known means posting it in a lot of places: Product Hunt, Show HN, BetaList, communities, review sites and hundreds of directories. Most of them have no API, or put a captcha in front of the form, so the posting itself stays yours. The **Launch** page keeps the list for you, writes the copy for each place, and remembers where you posted.

It is not a one day event. The list stays, and a place you can post to again (Product Hunt after a big update, a subreddit a few months later) comes back on its own.

## What your product is

The list is picked from about 800 places by what your product is: software or not, AI, for developers, open source, no code, sold to businesses, Bitcoin. The analysis of your website ticks those for you, and a linked GitHub repository adds **For developers**. Change them at the top of the **Launch** page; from then on they are yours, and a later analysis of your website no longer changes them.

A local business does not get software launch platforms; a product that is not built around AI does not get the AI directories; review sites like G2 and Capterra only come up for software sold to businesses. The subreddits your [target profiles](/product/discovery) found join the list too, marked **Found for you**.

Unticking a box drops the places it brought, except the ones you already posted to, wrote copy for or dismissed.

## The list

Places are grouped by kind: launch platforms, communities, review sites, deal marketplaces (lifetime deal sites like AppSumo, which take a share of each sale), then directories. Within each group the best known come first. Paid places carry a **Paid** badge and what they cost when it is known.

Three tabs: **To do**, **Posted** and **Dismissed**. Every place is optional: **Dismiss** the ones that are not for you, and **Restore** brings one back.

## Writing the copy

**Write copy** on a place drafts what to paste into its form, following that place's rules when they are known: a Product Hunt tagline of 60 characters and the maker's first comment, a Show HN title and text, a post that reads like a founder talking to a subreddit rather than an ad. Open the row to read the copy, edit it, and **Copy** it.

Most directories have no rules of their own and ask for the same things: a tagline, a short and a long description, and categories. That listing is written once, the first time you ask on a directory, and every other directory gets it at once, for free. **Rewrite copy** on a directory writes a new listing, which the directories you open next get.

## Marking a place posted

Post it yourself, then click **Mark posted** and paste the link to your post or listing. The link is required: it is how you find the post again, and the proof the step was done.

A place you can post to once stays posted. A place with a waiting period shows the date you can post again, and moves back to **To do** once it has passed, with your earlier posts kept on the row. Subreddits wait 90 days, Product Hunt six months.

## Adding a place

**Add a venue** puts a place the list does not have on your checklist: a niche directory, a newsletter, a community in your sector. Give its address and name, and optionally its kind, price, how many days before you can post there again, and its rules, which the copy will follow. Pasting the same address twice brings back the one you already added. Remove one with the bin icon; the posts you made there are kept.

## Evie and the API

[Evie](/product/evie) can do all of this from the chat: change what your product is, tell you what is left to do, write the copy for a place, mark one posted with its link, dismiss or restore one, and add a place. The [API](/product/api) serves the list and the tags too.
