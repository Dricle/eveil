# Proposing times from a reply

When somebody answers that they are interested, the next step is a call. Eveil drafts the answer offering times, but you always send the words yourself: no reply ever goes to a prospect on its own, whatever the [autonomy level](/product/autonomy).

## Set your meeting hours once

**Settings → Meetings** holds:

- **Time zone.** The zone your hours are in. It starts from your browser's.
- **A call lasts.** 15 minutes to an hour and a half.
- **Hours.** One row per window, for example Tuesday 10:00 to 12:00 and Thursday 14:00 to 16:00. Add two rows for a split day.
- **Where.** A video link, a phone number or an address. The draft puts it in the mail as you wrote it.

No calendar is connected. These hours are all Eveil knows about when you are free, so leave out the ones you are never available. Until the hours are set, the inbox shows a link to this page instead of the button below.

## Propose times

Open a conversation in the inbox and click **Propose times**. Eveil picks three times from your hours, one per day, starting tomorrow. It writes a short answer offering them, in the language of their reply and following the email box of **Settings → AI instructions**, and puts it in the reply box.

Nothing is sent. Read it, change what you like, and send it like any other reply. Each draft is one AI call.
