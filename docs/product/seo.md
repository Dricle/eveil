# SEO articles

The **SEO** page (its own item in the main navigation) holds articles written for your own blog: answers to what your buyers search for that your site doesn't answer yet.

## Where the topics come from

Every article starts from something Eveil already found, and says which one beside it:

- **A feature your site never explains.** Eveil compares the knowledge base against the pages it read on your site and the articles already written.
- **A competitor worth a comparison.** From the competitors in your knowledge base: an honest "X vs Y", never a hit piece.
- **A Reddit discussion worth an article.** While the [Reddit scan](/product/reddit) looks for threads to reply to, it also notes the ones that would make a good article, whether or not they're worth a reply.
- **A client you just won**, as a case study.
- **Industry news** with a real angle for your product.
- **Picked by Eveil**, when none of the above is strong enough: a topic your buyers search for that your site doesn't cover.

It never writes an article on something your site or a past article already covers. Articles are written in your site's language.

## Article ideas

The discussions the Reddit scan noted show up at the top of the SEO page as ideas, each with the angle the article would take and a link to the thread. **Write it** writes that one straight away. **Dismiss** removes it for good: the scan won't suggest the same thread again. Ideas you leave alone are also what the scheduled articles pick from.

## How often

Pick a cadence at the top of the SEO page: off (the default), daily, weekly, every two weeks or monthly. **Write one now** writes one straight away. You get an email when a scheduled article is ready, and it also shows on the Dashboard under "things to review".

You can also ask [Evie](/product/evie): tell her "we just shipped X" and she'll offer to write both a LinkedIn post and an article about it.

## Reviewing and publishing

Nothing publishes on its own: Eveil has no access to your blog. For each draft you can:

- **Copy** it as Markdown and paste it into your blog.
- **Edit** the title, the search snippet (meta description) or the text yourself.
- **Rework with Evie**: opens the chat on that article, so you can ask for changes in plain words ("shorter intro", "add a section on pricing"). She rewrites the draft in place.
- **Mark as published**, with the address it went live at. Eveil reads that page and won't write the same article again.
- **Reject** it, with an optional reason the writer takes into account next time, or delete it.
